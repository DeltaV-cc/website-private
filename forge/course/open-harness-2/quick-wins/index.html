Quick wins

Small, real things to run once your agent chats. Each one is copy-paste ready.

Clean your digital footprint — Unbroker

Data brokers republish your name, address and phone. The official Unbroker skill scans them and files removals autonomously, with your explicit consent per subject.

Steps · tap the number to check0/4
  1. Configure automatically, then check readiness.
  2. Verify the setup before registering anyone.
  3. Register yourself, with consent — it refuses to run without it.
  4. Run the removal loop until actions are exhausted.

Teach your agent a book — book-to-skill

Instead of pasting a whole PDF into context, book-to-skill ↗ distils a book into a skill: mental models, per-chapter files loaded on demand, a glossary and a cheatsheet — 24-51× fewer tokens than a raw dump.

Point at your screen — the HUD

With the HUD, you stop describing which window you mean. Put the bar over it and say "this" — the agent resolves the reference itself.

“You don't tell Hermes which app you mean anymore. Put the bar over it and say this.” — seen in the wild ↗
